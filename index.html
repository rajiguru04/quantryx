Hi, I'm Rajeswari! 👋

I write about Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) in India, product leadership, and what it truly takes to build a meaningful career. What you'll find here are my honest, unfiltered thoughts — no fluff, no hype.

For the past decade, I've built my career at the intersection of IT and Finance for global financial firms. During this time, I've had a front-row seat watching India transform from a primitive financial system into the world's leading digital financial economy.

To be honest, working for MNCs gave me a serious case of FOMO. I was watching all this incredible action happen right in my own backyard, but I also noticed a massive knowledge gap. While most of us use UPI daily (and thankfully rarely have to ask for loose change anymore! 😄), very few people actually understand the mechanics — or the risks — working behind the scenes.

I see a similar gap when it comes to professional growth. So many young professionals aren't intentional about their paths; they let their very first job accidentally shape their entire future, whether in IT, AI, or another field.

This blog is my space to demystify these topics. Whether you want to get smarter about your personal finances, grow as a product leader, or deliberately map out your next career move — you're in the right place. Welcome!
